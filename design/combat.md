# Combat

## Goal

Combat is the main event.

The game is a turn-based combat deckbuilder, but turns should feel active and satisfying rather than like a spreadsheet.

A good turn should feel like:

1. Read the enemy situation.
2. Plan a sequence.
3. Play cards.
4. Trigger effects and synergies.
5. Watch the character perform the actions.
6. Resolve the enemy turn.
7. Adapt the deck and strategy.

## Core systems

The current foundation includes:

- player HP
- Energy
- draw pile
- hand
- discard pile
- card costs
- attacks / defensive actions / utility actions
- enemy intent
- turns
- rewards
- run progression

## Card philosophy

Cards should represent things the character actually does in combat.

Avoid making every card feel like a generic number modifier.

Good cards should create decisions:

- Do I spend Energy now?
- Do I save a card for a sequence?
- Do I take a risky reward?
- Do I build around a specific effect?
- Do I remove a weaker card?
- Do I commit to a synergy?

## Vivienne

Vivienne's combat identity is precision and sequencing.

The player should be rewarded for arranging cards into useful sequences rather than simply playing the highest-numbered card available.

Her Blood resource adds another layer of decision-making.

Her signature ultimate is a major payoff for successful combat preparation.

## Presentation

Combat should have:

- clear hit feedback
- readable card effects
- satisfying animations
- strong character reactions
- clean enemy intent presentation
- dramatic ultimate presentation
- fast enough pacing that turns do not feel sluggish

No fake playtesting or fake combat results should ever be reported as verified.
