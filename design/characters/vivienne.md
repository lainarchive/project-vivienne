# Vivienne

## Concept

Vivienne is a vampire aristocrat and the first character in the roster.

She is elegant, confident, theatrical, and a little condescending. She treats combat almost like a performance.

She isn't emotionless. She is expressive, playful, and becomes noticeably more interested when an opponent proves capable.

## Personality

Core traits:

- charming
- confident
- witty
- competitive
- theatrical
- composed
- enjoys skilled opponents
- values style and precision

Her dialogue should be short and memorable rather than exposition-heavy.

Example tone:

> "Oh. You're actually interesting."

> "Do try to keep up."

> "That was almost impressive."

> "...Oh."

The exact dialogue is not locked.

## Visual identity

Current direction:

- vampire aristocrat
- pale, elegant presentation
- dark hair
- crimson eyes
- black / deep burgundy clothing
- Victorian-inspired details
- gloves
- high boots
- ornate accessories
- distinctive parasol
- dramatic gothic silhouette

The parasol is a signature visual element and a major part of her ultimate presentation.

## Combat identity

Vivienne is built around:

- precision
- sequences
- counters
- Blood
- escalating combo turns
- a signature parasol ultimate

The player should feel like they are learning a deliberate sequence-based playstyle.

## Ultimate

Working concept: a signature parasol ability.

When activated:

- the parasol opens
- the battlefield presentation changes
- Vivienne enters a temporary enhanced state
- her combat sequences become stronger
- the ultimate ends with a major visual payoff

Exact card, numbers, and effects are TBD.

## Design goal

Vivienne should be recognizable from her silhouette and personality alone.

She should also establish the game's overall standard for character presentation without defining every character as a vampire clone.
